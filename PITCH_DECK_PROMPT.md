### Slide 2: The Core Problem
- **Slide Title**: The Hidden Cost of Informal Trade
- **Key Metric Highlight**: **₦120B+** lost annually in uncollected customer debt & untracked cash flow.
- **Visual Recommendation**: 3 problem cards with distinct warning badges (Uncollected Debt, Opaque Cash Flow, Zero Creditworthiness).
- **Slide Copy (Short & Precise)**:
  - **Lost Revenue**: Over 30% of sales happen on credit; paper ledgers get damaged, forgotten, or disputed.
  - **Distorted Margins**: Borrowed cash and credit repayments get mixed up with daily sales, creating false profit illusions.
  - **Financial Exclusion**: Informal merchants remain invisible to commercial banks because they lack verified digital records.
- **Why It Sells**:
  - Clearly defines the monetary pain: Traders aren't looking for "software"; they are looking to stop losing money on unpaid customer debt and inventory stockouts.
- **Speaker Notes**:
  > *"When a trader gives out goods or cash on trust, it usually goes into a tattered notebook under the counter. If that book gets lost or wet, their money vanishes. Worse, merchants often mistake debt repayment for new profit, leading them to overspend and run out of capital. MarketPulse turns chaos into crystal-clear cash visibility."*

### Slide 5: The Financial Innovation — Smart Credit Netting
- **Slide Title**: Accurate Accounting: The Debt Recovery Engine
- **Visual Recommendation**: Balance comparison diagram showing the flaw of traditional apps vs. MarketPulse's Netting Engine.
- **Slide Copy (Short & Precise)**:
  - **The Double-Counting Trap Solved**: Traditional apps count debt repayment as new sales, artificially inflating revenue.
  - **Automatic Expense Offset**: When a debtor refunds money, MarketPulse directly deducts it from **Money Out** and restores true balance.
  - **One-Tap WhatsApp Reminders**: Send professional, cooldown-guarded payment reminders directly to customer phones.
- **Why It Sells**:
  - Highlights deep operational domain knowledge. Shows the product was built with real merchant feedback, solving real ledger math rather than generic CRUD.
- **Speaker Notes**:
  > *"When a trader lends ₦10,000 and the customer pays it back next week, that is NOT ₦10,000 of new sales revenue. If you record it as income, your profit numbers lie to you. MarketPulse implements a unique Debt Recovery Netting Engine: it reduces your 'Money Out', restores your cash balance, and keeps your true sales unpolluted."*
